# Reports

This folder holds reports that the Notes keeper writes about your notes.

- A tidy-up report suggests which pages to merge, split, or shorten, which pages look old, and which links are missing.
- A report is named with its date: `2026-01-31-refine.md`.
- A report changes nothing else. You decide which suggestions to follow. Ask the Notes keeper to follow one, or make the change yourself.
