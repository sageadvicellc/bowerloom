## Choose a workspace and profile

Use [Prepare your first team](/docs/learn/first-team/) for an unused target. Use [Existing project](/docs/guides/existing-project/) for an existing directory.

The fixed profiles are Engineer, Founder, and Research. They prepare role templates without asking a model to design a team.

## Review the plan

Each task shows its exact planning command and full file-review step. The target, goal, profile, and binding remain part of approval.

## Approve the exact files

Use only the revision from the human's reviewed plan. After application, follow [Read and review your setup](/docs/learn/review-your-setup/).

## Share definitions, not private state

Read [Files and configuration](/docs/configuration/) before sharing. Keep private receipts, local paths, journals, logs, and access details private.
