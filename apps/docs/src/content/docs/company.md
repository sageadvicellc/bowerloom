## Personal work

A personal setup prepares definitions for your existing agent. Optional local connections and registered controls require separate plans and approvals.

Local setup does not establish unattended autonomy. Review the actual owner, scope, and stop behavior before running work.

## Shared company work

The planned first company trial uses cast-based dummy accounts, dummy emails, and synthetic project data. These are test identities, not employees with real access.

Beta acceptance includes company-server access controls, personal autonomy, semantic retrieval, offboarding, deletion, and two harnesses. The installed setup trial does not satisfy these gates.

Real business data needs an approved retention and deletion policy. Do not infer shared-service readiness from a successful local installation.

## Evidence and limits

These are required beta boundaries recorded in the [engineering plan](https://github.com/sageadvicellc/bowerloom/blob/37f1efab545921d08378956eef854c3b17bd4f16/docs/beta/engineering-plan.md), snapshot `37f1efa`. This page offers no company-server provisioning procedure or accepted real-user trial.
