## Read results at their actual scope

Specification readiness means the installed files meet their checks. It is not runtime readiness or permission to execute a team.

Synthetic checks use controlled inputs and test identities. They do not qualify arbitrary applications, connected services, or native tool containment.

Review the installed version, environment, exact approval, and capability-specific requirements before each operation. [Permissions](/docs/permissions/) and [security limits](/docs/security/) explain these boundaries.
