# Persona report format

Each persona writes ONE report. Write it in the first person, in plain English. Keep it under 700 words. Use no tables.

## First 60 seconds

Say what you thought the app was for. Say what you clicked first, and why.

## Tasks

Give one plain list item for each of the ten tasks, in order. Each item has five parts:

- The task, in a few words.
- The result: done, stuck or gave up.
- How many tries you made.
- How long it felt, counted as the number of commands you ran.
- A short quote of the words that confused you. If nothing confused you, write "none".

## Top 5 problems

Rank the five worst problems as HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW. For each one, give four parts:

- The screen where it happened.
- What you expected.
- What happened instead.
- One concrete fix.

## What worked

Name the things that felt good. Be specific.

## Would I keep using it? Why

Answer yes, no or not yet. Give your reasons in two or three sentences.

## Rules for every report

- Quote what you saw. Do not guess what the code does.
- When the window does not show the answer, say "I cannot tell".
- Do not name files, folders or source code. You never read them.
- Stay in character from start to end.
