# Milestones and review

Milestone one: review the explicit brief, proposed scope, owners, permissions, and acceptance criteria. Resolve unknowns before implementation.

Milestone two: review a proposed draft against the accepted scope. Require evidence for factual claims and label missing information.

Milestone three: review the independent critique and decide whether to revise or accept the deliverable.

Selected review cadence: milestones. Present each proposed milestone to the user.

Nothing ran during startup. Compilation proves the specification shape and asset pins only. It does not prove quality, feasibility, runtime readiness, or founder acceptance.
